Frequently asked questions

What is the price of the XPeng G6 in Indonesia?

Saat ini, XPeng G6 Pro Rp679.000.000 dan XPeng G6 AWD Rp769.000.000. Hubungi kami untuk konfirmasi harga terbaru.

What is the range of the XPeng G6 Pro?

Up to about 525 km on the WLTP standard with the 80.8 kWh LFP battery.

What is the difference between the G6 Pro and G6 AWD?

The G6 Pro uses a single rear-wheel-drive motor. The G6 AWD has an extra motor at the front, so its power is much higher, with a slightly shorter range.
