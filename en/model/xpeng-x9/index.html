Can I test drive the XPeng X9?

What is the difference between the latest XPeng X9 and the old X9?

The latest X9 uses a 110 kWh NCM battery (previously 101.5 kWh) with a range of up to 715 km NEDC (previously 690 km). Its peak DC charging power is 542 kW (previously 317 kW), so 10–80% charging takes about 12 minutes (previously 20 minutes). The design is also refreshed, with added features such as a head-up display, Road Noise Cancellation, and Flat Tire Stability Control. Contact us for details and a test drive schedule.
