History

The company's journey

  1. 1992-2006

    The Beginnings of PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk

    Erajaya's journey began in 1992 from a small shop on Jalan Rawa Bahagia 1 No. 12, Grogol, West Jakarta. Four years later, in 1996, the company was officially established with a focus on distributing and retailing technology products, especially mobile telecommunications devices. From there, Erajaya grew into an importer, distributor, and retailer of mobile devices recognized as one of the largest and most trusted in Indonesia.

  2. 2007-2016

    Building Retail and Going Public

    In 2007, Erajaya opened its first erafone store, which later became the backbone of its retail network. In 2011, PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk was listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (ticker ERAA). The company then expanded through acquisitions, namely iBox in 2012. In 2014, Erajaya acquired CG Computer Sdn Bhd in Malaysia, owner of the Switch brand, an Apple Premium Reseller and Apple Authorised Reseller, with 23 retail stores by the end of that year. In 2016, the first Urban Republic store in Indonesia opened.

  3. 2018-2019

    The Omni Channel Era

    In 2018, Erajaya Group launched its Omni Channel service, integrating offline and online business through O2O services. Its network includes erafone, iBox, Urban Republic, Samsung Experience Store, and erafone.com. In 2019, Eraspace was launched, an omnichannel retail platform that unites online and offline shopping, together with the MyEraspace loyalty program to strengthen customer engagement.

  4. 2021-Present

    Diversifying Through Three Business Verticals

    In 2021, Erajaya formed three business verticals: Erajaya Digital, Erajaya Active Lifestyle, and Erajaya Food & Nourishment. This step transformed Erajaya from a mobile device retailer into a lifestyle retail and distribution ecosystem bringing many global brands to Indonesia.   9. 2024

    Current Position

    As of 30 September 2024, Erajaya had 42 distribution centers, 2,099 retail stores, and about 54,000 third-party outlets billed directly, spread across Indonesia, Singapore, and Malaysia. Besides mobile device distribution and retail, the company offers value-added services such as TecProtec phone protection and handset leasing. Its head office is at Erajaya Plaza, Jl.
